## Current implementation and next evidence

The service consumes an explicit, short-lived start ticket and fresh recorded consent, then uses the buffered PCM coordinator. Service-level pending stop/discard latches also cover cancellation while a source factory is preparing. The coordinator retains its local published stop intent across a concurrent start-slot reset. Notification controls remain session-scoped. Meeting deletion closes admission and discards pending PCM before waiting for the persistent privacy barrier; already-running I/O is still governed by that durable barrier. The final interleaving regression preserves the admitted 800-sample prefix for ordinary end, while privacy discard clears the queued samples, commits no frame and records the gap.

The next physical evidence needs a selected phone/build/input configuration and an explicitly permitted recording scenario. The development gate must remain closed until the applicable software requirements and physical evidence are complete. Synthetic FGS success is not a reason to enable the microphone. Long-duration power/thermal behavior, actual output routing, forensic flash erasure and scheduled-cleanup deadlines are not established.

Independent control/storage executors remain in place, and the source reader now hands data to a bounded single consumer. At 16 kHz mono the queue owns at most 32,000 samples / 64,000 PCM bytes including in-flight data, excluding other buffers. The first stop boundary, paused sealing limit and known rejected-callback gap commit together. Recovery records an unknown tail after the committed prefix when the RAM boundary is unavailable; it does not invent a duration or resume recording.

With trusted time, audio expiry first commits new deletion ranges and wrapped-key revocation, then retries old ciphertext cleanup. The static scoped audio-maintenance entry avoids constructor recovery; ordinary store opens still fully recover. A clock failure permits only retry of existing revocations and still reports failure. Cancellation before commit rolls the transaction back; later cleanup failure cannot reverse committed revocation. Note access checks a complete clock anchor before body reads/decryption and again before return; audio reads also recheck time and revocation before returning PCM. A failed initial preference commit remains pending by storage-root and preference-identity indexes until the original anchor is successfully retried. Confirmed-anchor reads use same-boot monotonic projection without checkpoint rewrites; larger wall jumps are quarantined. The expanded audio-maintenance path's 107 checks passed within the earlier `8B3E32FF` native suite; current-candidate Android regression, physical durability and cleanup deadlines remain unverified.

Pages do storage/cryptography off the main thread. Confirmed mutations remain authorized in the queue after departure; reads and late UI updates still obey epoch/visibility barriers, and store access, expiry, deletion and revision checks remain in force. Executor shutdown drains submitted work rather than promising durability across process death. The recording page does not overwrite its saving state with live telemetry. Its unknown-tail warning is bounded by NoteStore visibility lifetime and recovery-query checks before and after reading. Preparation avoids historical-title decryption. Development text-policy snapshots do not establish user-selected production policy. Independent control TTL, platform driver stalls, native loss, actual power interruption, complete downstream gap presentation, production TTL, declaration deadlines and trusted cross-boot recovery remain open. Earlier software dispatch observations are historical and are not hardware stopping guarantees.
